Privacy Policy

Bewafa Community, Discord Bots & SMP

Last Updated: Current

🛡️ Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Bewafa Community ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your data across our Discord Server, our custom Discord Bots (including Zyrox and SMP Bridge Bots), and our Minecraft Survival Multiplayer (SMP) server.

By joining our server, using our bots, or playing on our SMP, you consent to the data practices described in this document. We are committed to minimizing data collection to only what is required to provide a seamless and safe community experience.

📦 Information We Collect

Discord Data

When you interact with our Discord server or authenticate with our bots, we may collect the following via the Discord API:

  • User IDs & Profile Info: Your unique Discord ID, username, global name, and avatar.
  • Message Content: Chat messages sent in specific bridge channels (to sync with Minecraft) or command inputs triggered for our bots.
  • Server Data: Your roles, join date, and voice channel activity (used for internal XP and event tracking).

Minecraft SMP Data

If you connect to our Minecraft SMP, we process:

  • Minecraft UUIDs & Usernames: To identify your account in-game.
  • Connection Data: IP addresses are temporarily processed for security, anti-DDoS, and preventing ban evasion.
  • Account Linking: Data connecting your Discord ID to your Minecraft UUID (via DiscordSRV) to sync chat and roles.

⚙️ How We Use Your Data

We do not collect data for tracking or advertising. Your data is strictly used for the operational functionality of the community:

  • Cross-Platform Syncing: Relaying chat messages and server status between the Discord server and the Minecraft SMP in real-time.
  • Bot Functionality: Processing commands, awarding XP, managing economies, and facilitating server events/giveaways via our custom bots.
  • Moderation & Security: Maintaining logs to protect the server from spam, griefing, and malicious actors.

🚫 AI & Machine Learning: We absolutely do not use your message content, voice data, or personal information to train AI models, LLMs, or any automated external systems.

🤖 Discord Privileged Intents

Our custom bots utilize Discord Privileged Intents strictly to function properly. Here is why we need them:

  • Message Content Intent: Required to read messages sent in the Discord-to-Minecraft bridge channel so they can be broadcasted in-game, and to listen for specific text-based commands or automoderation triggers.
  • Server Members Intent: Required to verify when a user joins or leaves, assign automatic roles, sync Minecraft whitelist statuses, and keep our internal XP/economy databases up to date.

Message content is held in temporary memory to execute the relay or command and is discarded immediately. We do not maintain a permanent database of your Discord chat history.

🔒 Data Storage & Sharing

Storage: Registration data (like account linking) and basic economy/XP data are stored securely on Bewafa's server infrastructure provided by our hosts. We employ standard security measures to prevent unauthorized access.

Sharing: Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers. The only entities data is shared with are the platforms themselves (Discord and Mojang/Microsoft) as required to make the integrations work.

⚖️ Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain full control over your data within our ecosystem. You have the right to:

  • Request Access: View the profile/economy data our bots have saved about you.
  • Unlink Accounts: Disconnect your Discord account from your Minecraft profile at any time.
  • Request Deletion: You may request the total deletion of your profile data from our bot databases.

To request data deletion, open a support ticket in the Discord server or contact the development team directly.
